A little message from the village

Happy Birthday, Sayanika!

To the most unexpected and now the most cherished friend,❤️
Initially plan chhilo ekta boro-shoro message likhbo, but honestly, I don't even know koto boro hoye jabe.

Last winter jokhon ami toke Dum Dum station-e prothom dekhte peyechhilam, I was genuinely so excited! Cuz ami toke school-e jerom chhere eshechhilam, tui ekdom serom-i chhili (physically, at least). The same muffler jorano meye, jaake ami roj bus-e uthte dekhtam... puro bus-er sobcheye serious passenger! 😂

Tui hoyto bhabchhis je ami nishchoi kono ek maratok golper buildup korchhi, but na. Karon ami jodi sei golpo bolte jai, tahole birthday wish-er theke love story beshi shonabe. 😂

Tor mone hotei pare, "koto dhit chele re baba!" And honestly, ami nijeo jani. But I don't want to jinx anything (touchwood bhai!), ami tor opor give up korchhi na. Conversation hok ba na hok, kom hok ba beshi, I genuinely wish jeno tor pashe thakte pari.

Jani ekhon khub chap, aar somoy sobsomoy bhalo jay na. But aajker dine sob chap ektu side-e rekhe enjoy kor, smile kor. After all, silver jubilee year bhai! 🥳

Ar please, negative ba pessimistic kichhu bhabish na. Jibone ja ja face korechhis, jevabe nijeke samle niyechhis, je comeback-gulo diyechhis, aar ekhono jevabe nijer jonno khat-chhis, seta genuinely commendable. Maybe tui nije sobsomoy realise koris na, but you should be proud of yourself.

I hope the coming year brings you more reasons to smile, more peace of mind and all the happiness you deserve.

Ar haan, dekha kor ekdin. Cuz tor theke fuchka khawa aar toke fuchka khawano, dutoi ekhono baki ache! 😂 **Have the happiest birthday, Sayanika! Stay the way you are. ❤️**

You are loved, today and every day. 💗
